# Editorial policy

ARG Data follows evidence, not political outcomes.

## Source hierarchy
1. Primary official dataset/document.
2. Original international institution dataset/document.
3. Academic or methodological source.
4. High-quality secondary reporting for context only.

## Mandatory checks
- stock vs flow
- gross vs net
- nominal vs real
- monthly vs YoY vs QoQ
- seasonally adjusted vs trend-cycle vs raw
- revisions and release dates
- denominator/base effects
- causation vs correlation

## Publication
V0 never auto-publishes political/economic verdicts. A human must approve the evidence package. Material contradictions block publication until resolved.
