# ARG Data Editorial Policy — V0

## Purpose

ARG Data verifies factual claims about Argentina using reproducible evidence. It is not an advocacy system for a government, opposition, party, candidate, media organization, or ideology.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary statistical/administrative source.
2. Primary methodology/documentation.
3. International organization with identifiable methodology.
4. Secondary reporting for discovery/context only.

A secondary article must not become the sole evidence for a numeric verdict when a primary source is reasonably available.

## Claim decomposition

Compound claims must be split before verdicting. Numeric premises and causal conclusions are separate propositions. A correct number does not make an inference based on that number correct.

## Comparability checks

Before comparing values, verify at minimum:

- stock vs flow;
- gross vs net;
- nominal vs real;
- monthly vs quarterly vs annual;
- seasonally adjusted vs unadjusted vs trend-cycle;
- public debt vs total external debt;
- level vs change;
- current vintage vs revised historical series;
- same currency/unit and reference period.

## Verdicts

- `TRUE`: material claim supported by evidence without important missing context.
- `MOSTLY_TRUE`: core claim supported; limited qualification needed.
- `MISLEADING`: uses real/partly real evidence but framing, comparison, omitted context or inference materially changes the meaning.
- `MOSTLY_FALSE`: core proposition contradicted, though some supporting elements are correct.
- `FALSE`: material proposition contradicted by reliable evidence.
- `UNVERIFIABLE`: available evidence cannot responsibly establish a verdict.

## Confidence

Confidence is confidence in the evidence/verdict, not probability that the speaker is dishonest.

No claim about intent is inferred from factual incorrectness.

## Publication gate

During V0 all political, macroeconomic, fiscal, debt, poverty, employment and election-related fact checks require explicit human approval.

Automatic discovery and drafting are permitted. Automatic public confrontation or posting is not.

## Corrections

Corrections must preserve an audit trail. A revised official series can change a verdict; ARG Data should show the revision rather than silently overwrite the historical record.

## Independence rule

The radar score cannot include whether a potential verdict is favorable or unfavorable to the current government or opposition.
